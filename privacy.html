Privacy Policy

Tiny Kingdom Siege · Effective 17 November 2026

Tiny Kingdom Siege (“the game”) is made by Tej, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: tejpalsharma771@gmail.com.

The short version: there are no accounts and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. The game shows ads and measures how it is played, using AppLovin (ads) and Google Firebase (game statistics and crash reports). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. You can turn analytics off in the game’s Settings and decline tracking when iOS asks.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, upgrades), settings and purchases you own are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data. Purchases can be brought back with Restore Purchases in the game’s Shop.

2. Advertising (AppLovin MAX)

The game shows optional reward videos (for example “×3 coins” or “Revive”) and, from level 6, occasional full-screen ads between battles. Ads are served by AppLovin and the ad networks it works with. To show, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, they may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, and the vendor identifier (IDFV);
  • device and connection details: model, operating system, language, time zone, IP address and coarse location derived from it;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, watched or tapped.

If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the game works exactly the same; ads are simply not personalised using your IDFA.

3. Game analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard and how the game can be improved, we record gameplay events: levels started, won or lost (with the level number and how long it took), ads shown and completed, purchases made, and session length. Firebase also receives device model, operating system, country and a random app-instance identifier. If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (where in the game it crashed, device model, OS version, free memory) so we can fix it. This data is not used to identify you.

Provider: Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). You can switch both off at any time in Settings → Analytics.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We receive only which item was bought, its price and currency, so the game can unlock it and count sales.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: answer the iOS prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Analytics: turn off in the game’s Settings.
  • Access or deletion: email us and we will ask Google to delete analytics data linked to your device’s identifiers. Ad data can be managed through AppLovin’s privacy page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Contact us to use them. Our legal bases are your consent (tracking) and our legitimate interest in running and improving a free, ad-supported game.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and security

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months, crash reports for 90 days, and ad data according to AppLovin’s policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

9. Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date at the top.
